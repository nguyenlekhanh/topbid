OBJECTIVE:
Provide the ability to determine the previous highest bidder for a category as required by PROJECT_PLAN.md ("6.1 | Detect previous highest bidder") - the detection primitive Phase 6 outbid-notification tasks will consume.

INTERPRETATION (derived strictly from plan/schema):
"Previous highest bidder" = the holder of the top PAID bid for a category excluding a given bid - i.e., who was on top immediately before another bid became the new #1. Ranking semantics are the established ones (paid bids only; amount DESC with created_at DESC tie-breaker). It is DERIVED from authoritative paid-bid history on demand; no new stored state, ledger table, or RPC is required.

IMPLEMENTATION NOTES:
Query shape: .from('bids').select(BID_FIELDS).eq('category_id', categoryId).eq('status','paid').neq('id', excludeBidId).order('amount DESC').order('created_at DESC').limit(1).maybeSingle(). Guards: blank/whitespace categoryId or excludeBidId -> null without querying (consistent trim-based guards used by sibling validators). Null contract: no other paid bids -> null (the excluded/new bid was the first); refunds naturally remove a former champion from results (never notify about refunded payments - documented). Deliberately NOT an RPC inside Task 4.8's conversion: paid history is immutable so detection is derivable at any later moment; capturing it at conversion time would couple notification plumbing into the payment path for no correctness gain. Live DB verification remains unavailable (no Docker) - deterministic unit tests cover mapping/guards instead.
